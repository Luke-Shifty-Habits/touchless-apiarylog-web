Hive notes you say,
not type.

Gloves on, smoker in one hand, hive tool in the other. Press one button, talk through the inspection, press again. Apiary Log transcribes it on the phone — no signal needed — and files each note under the right hive.

One take, many hives

"Hive 1, no brood. Hive 2, eggs, brood and larvae. Hive 7, queen spotted."

Hive 1no brood
Hive 2eggs, brood and larvae
Hive 7queen spotted

Anything said before the first hive becomes a note for the apiary itself.

Built for the apiary

Works with no reception

Speech recognition runs entirely on your phone. Once the speech model is downloaded (once, at home), the app never needs the internet.

Glove-sized controls

The record button fills the screen. The volume keys start and stop a take too, and the phone buzzes so you know it heard you.

Works from the lock screen

Leave it open, lock the phone, pocket it. Wake it and the record button is right there — no unlocking with gloves on.

Asks before it guesses

Mention a hive it doesn't know, or one recorded at another apiary, and it asks out loud: "No Hive 12 in the app. Create?" Say yes, or tap the big button.

Your words, kept

The original recording and exactly what was heard are always kept. Correct a note later and the original stays beside your edit.

Hives that move

Hive numbers are unique across all your apiaries. Move Hive 7 to a new site and its history comes with it, each note still showing where it was made.

Private by design

No account, no cloud, no ads, no tracking. Your recordings, transcripts and notes stay on your phone. Export them to a spreadsheet — to Google Drive or anywhere you like — whenever you want a copy. Privacy policy.

What you'll need

  1. An Android phone (Android 8 or newer, 64-bit — most phones from the last several years).
  2. About 1 GB free for the app and its speech model.
  3. Wi-Fi once, to download the speech model (about 660 MB) from the app's settings.

Apiary Log is in testing now. A Bluetooth button for true hands-free use is on the way.
