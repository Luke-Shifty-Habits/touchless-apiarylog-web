Privacy policy

Apiary Log for Android (package com.touchless.apiarylog). Effective 20 November 2026.

In short: Your recordings, transcripts and notes are created and kept on your phone. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics, and we never sell or share your data. If the app crashes, a technical crash report is sent so the bug can be fixed; it never contains your notes or recordings, and you can turn it off. The only time anything of yours leaves the phone is if you turn on voice-sample sharing, which is off unless you choose it (see below).

What the app stores, and where

Everything below is stored in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read:

  • Audio recordings of your inspections.
  • Transcripts of those recordings, and any corrections you make.
  • Your apiaries, hives and notes, and your settings.
  • The speech-recognition model, once you download it.

None of it is sent to us or to anyone else, except the voice samples you choose to share.

Speech recognition happens on your phone

Transcription runs entirely on the device. Your voice is never sent to a speech service, and the app works with no internet connection at all — including in airplane mode.

When the app uses the internet

Recording, transcription, questions and notes never use the internet. The app uses it only for these four things, and each one simply does nothing when you have no reception:

  • Downloading the speech model. When you tap Download under Settings → Speech model, the app downloads the speech-recognition model files (about 660 MB) from their public hosts: Hugging Face (huggingface.co) and GitHub (github.com). As with any download, those services can see your device's IP address and the file requested. Nothing about you or your notes is sent.
  • Checking for updates. If you installed the app from Google Play, it asks Google Play whether a newer version is available, using Google Play's own in-app update service. The request is made by Google Play, under Google's privacy policy; the app sends nothing of its own.
  • Crash reports (on by default, can be turned off). If the app crashes, a crash report is saved on the phone and sent the next time the app opens with a connection, using Google's Firebase Crashlytics. It contains the technical details of the crash (where in the app's code it happened), facts about the device (model, Android version, free memory), the app version, the stage the app was at (for example "recording") and a random installation ID created by Firebase. It never contains your notes, transcripts, hive numbers, apiary names or recordings. Reports are used only to find and fix bugs, and are deleted automatically after 90 days. Turn them off in Settings → Advanced → Send crash reports.
  • Voice samples, only if you turn them on. Described in the next section.

Voice samples (optional, off unless you turn it on)

Under Settings → Help improve recognition you can choose to share recordings so the app's speech recognition can be improved for beekeepers. It is off by default, and nothing is shared unless you switch it on.

  • What is sent: for each take recorded after you switch it on, the audio recording, its transcript and any corrections you made, the time it was recorded, its length, and the app and speech-model versions.
  • What is never sent: your apiary names, hive numbers or details, inspection stats, or anything else from the app. No name, email or account is attached; each install is identified only by a random anonymous ID.
  • When: only over Wi-Fi or another unmetered connection, once you have checked the take's notes, or after 7 days if you have not. Takes that were never transcribed, or that you deleted, are not sent.
  • Where: to Google Cloud Storage, through a Firebase project used only for this app (Firebase Anonymous Authentication and Cloud Storage, plus Crashlytics for the crash reports above; no Firebase Analytics). It may be stored in a data centre outside your country. Google processes it on our behalf under Firebase's privacy and security terms.
  • How it is used: only by us, only to improve and test the app's speech recognition. It is never sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.
  • Stopping and deleting: switch the setting off and anything still waiting is discarded. To delete what was sent, see Deleting your Apiary Log data.
  • Retention: samples are kept until you delete them or until they are no longer needed for improving recognition.

Voice recordings can contain anything said nearby, so please avoid sharing takes where other people can be heard without their agreement.

Exports

You can export your notes as a spreadsheet, or as a ZIP file with the recordings. The app writes the file to a location you choose in Android's own file picker — for example Google Drive, your Downloads folder or a USB drive. What happens to that file afterwards is governed by where you saved it (for example Google's own privacy policy, if you choose Google Drive).

Deleting your Apiary Log data

Apiary Log has no accounts, so there is nothing to sign in to or close. Almost everything is stored only on your phone, and you delete it there.

To delete your voice samples

  1. Open Apiary Log and go to Settings.
  2. Under Help improve recognition, tap Delete my samples, then tap again to confirm.
  3. Every sample sent from this install is deleted from our storage straight away. Turn off Send voice samples to stop sending new ones.

If you have already uninstalled the app, email Shiftyhabits@gmail.com and tell us roughly when you recorded them. Samples carry no name or account, so we will delete those matching the dates you give.

To stop crash reports

  1. Open Apiary Log and go to Settings → Advanced.
  2. Turn off Send crash reports.

To delete everything on your phone

Delete a note in the app (open it, then Delete) and it is removed, along with its recording once no other note uses it. Uninstalling Apiary Log deletes all of its notes, recordings, hives, apiaries, settings and the speech model. The app does not use Android's cloud backup, so no copy is kept anywhere else. Export files you saved yourself are not affected.
